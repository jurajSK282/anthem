# Emberwood — scene brief for the game director

_First area of Anthem. State as of 2026-11-14. This describes what the player sees and feels, not how it is
built; the technical side is in `STATE.md`._

## One-line pitch

A foggy autumn forest at dusk, lit from behind by a huge low moon: black trunks against amber haze, with small
orange lights and glowing flowers leading the player along a sunken path to a dark lake.

## Mood and references

- **Feeling:** quiet, melancholy, slightly uneasy. Warm colours, but a dark and lonely place.
- **Style:** early-2000s first-person RPG (Gothic 1/2, Dark Messiah). Low-poly shapes, small pixelated
  textures, strong fog. Atmosphere carries the scene, not detail.
- **Contrast idea (from Elden Ring):** a dark, muted world where a few bright objects on the ground catch the
  eye and pull the player forward.

## Palette

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Sky and fog | amber to burnt orange, darker brown overhead |
| Trees, shadows | near-black brown silhouettes |
| Ground | dark umber soil, burnt sienna and yellow ochre leaves |
| Accents | glowing orange and yellow: mushrooms, flowers, floating leaves, fireflies |
| Stone | warm grey |
| Water | near black, picking up the amber haze in the distance |

There is no green and no blue anywhere in the scene.

## Layout

- **Size:** 180 x 180 m of rolling forest. Fog hides everything beyond about 50 m.
- **Path:** one sunken, winding dirt path about 160 m long, climbing gently towards the moon. Walking it takes
  about 45 seconds.
- **Lake:** an irregular lake about 50 m wide and 80 m long, on the right of the path through its middle
  stretch. A small island with a large bush sits in it; a low, thick-trunked mossy tree stands on the near shore.
- **Start:** the player spawns at the low southern end of the path, facing along it into the light.

## What the player sees along the way

1. **The start.** Tall dark trunks on both banks, a few strands of moss trailing from the branches overhead, the path dropping away ahead, clusters of glowing mushrooms along the
   edges, fallen leaves drifting down, fireflies near the ground.
2. **The forest floor.** Soil with scattered leaves on the path, thicker leaf litter and bright ochre drifts
   off it. Bushes and ferns grow in patches, with a few large bushes. Stones lie along the path edges, with
   bigger rocks, slabs and standing stones deeper in.
3. **Flower patches.** Clusters of small glowing star-shaped flowers, some beside the path and some glimpsed
   between distant trunks.
4. **The lake.** The trees open up and are heavily draped with moss here. Dark rippling water, patches of glowing orange leaves floating in the
   shallows, flowers along the shore and a broken streak of moonlight across the surface. The moon is clearly
   visible here, large and cratered, behind the far treeline.
5. **Beyond the lake.** The path continues uphill to the edge of the map. Nothing marks the end yet.

## Light

The only large light is the low moon behind the forest, so everything is back-lit and the player mostly walks
towards it. Local light comes from the glowing mushrooms and flower patches, which tint the ground around them.

## What is placeholder

- **Trees:** generated shapes, to be replaced by proper low-poly models.
- **No sound, no creatures, no interaction.** The player can only walk and look.
- **The map edge is open:** the terrain simply stops.

## Planned next

- A larger forest (about four times the area), with a proper boundary and possibly a fork in the path.
- A landmark to walk towards.

## Questions for the director

- What is the landmark, and where should the path end: at the lake, or somewhere beyond it?
- Should the lake be a place to stop (something to find there) or only scenery to pass?
- What do the glowing mushrooms and flowers mean in the world: natural plants, something magical, a warning?
- How dark should it get? The current look is deliberately gloomy; brighter would show more of the colour.
- Is the forest empty on purpose at this point, or should the player meet something here?
