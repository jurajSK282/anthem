# Project state (update at the end of every work block)

_Last updated: 2026-11-14_

## Current focus
Atmosphere and look of the first area ("Emberwood"), then first-person combat. Iterative, no fixed end scope.

## What exists
- **Emberwood scene** — generated entirely by editor scripts in `Assets/_Project/Editor/Emberwood/`
  (`EmberwoodBuilder` orchestrates). Menu `Anthem/Build Emberwood` wipes and rebuilds the scene (exit Play mode first;
  hand edits are lost). Autumn-ember forest: fog, glow bulbs, fireflies, haze/leaf particles.
  - `EmberTerrain` — Unity Terrain (180 m, 129 heightmap) with 4 noise-blended layers: soil, litter, drift, path.
    Use `EmberTerrain.Ss` for GLSL-style smoothstep; `Mathf.SmoothStep` is a different function.
  - `EmberFoliage` — alpha cards merged per 45 m chunk, 4 submeshes: dark bush, bright bush, fern A, fern B.
    Ferns grow in their own patches; ~6% of bush clumps are large dome bushes made of many normal-sized cards.
  - `EmberRocks` — scatters the 18 stone FBX from `Art/Stones/` (user-made in Blender): pebbles on path edges,
    medium huddles on banks, boulders/menhirs in the forest; seated on the sampled terrain surface.
  - Lake: `EmberTerrain` carves a shallow, irregular elliptical basin beside the path, about 50 x 80 m, with a small
    island (`Lake`, `LakeRx/Rz`, `Island`, `WaterY`, `LakeQ()`, `Wet()`); trees, foliage,
    bulbs and flowers skip wet ground. `EmberLake` adds the water disc and emissive floating leaf patches. Water uses
    `Anthem/Water` (haze fresnel, scrolling ripples, moon glitter, point-light glints, depth-fade shoreline; it
    turns on the URP camera depth texture). Water is ~0.7 m deep, so the player wades.
  - `EmberFlowers` — 64 patches of small emissive orange star-shaped flowers (contrast objects) on the lake shore,
    beside the path and through the forest, one merged mesh plus a dim point light per patch. Textures from `Tools/Textures/lake_textures.py`.
  - `EmberLakeProps` — large bush on the island and a low, thick-trunked mossy tree on the west shore.
  - Moss: one hanging card per strand (never crossed). Normal trees carry a few strands; the six `mossy` tree
    variants (seeds 200+) are draped and are used wherever `LakeQ < 1.9`, so moss concentrates at the lake.
  - Moon: `Anthem/GradientSky` draws a hard-edged textured moon disc (`_MoonDir`, `_MoonSize`, `_MoonColor`,
    `T_MJ_Moon` 64 px), set in `EmberAtmosphere`. It is sky-only; the directional light is unchanged.
  - `EmberMushrooms` — glowing low-poly mushrooms (4 mesh variants, stem + emissive cap) in clusters with a
    point light each: 46 along the path, up to 36 through the forest. They replaced the crystal bulbs.
  - `EmberMeshes` / `EmberScatter` — procedural trees, particles. Tree meshes have 3 submeshes:
    bark, leaf cards, hanging moss/vine cards (`T_MJ_Hanging` atlas from `Tools/Textures/hanging_texture.py`). `EmberAtmosphere` — light, fog, volume.
  - Textures in `Art/Emberwood/MJ/`: ground layers `T_L_*` and rock `T_MJ_RockLight` are generated by
    `Tools/Textures/ground_layers.py <sprite folder>` and `rock_texture.py` (seamless, no mirroring); leaf cards,
    bark and ferns come from the user's Midjourney images. User source sprites: `Desktop/scenes/textures/`.
  - Player with `FirstPersonMotor` is built into the scene at the start of the path.
- `AnthemPipelineSetup.cs` — URP/pipeline setup.
- **PoC** (`Assets/PoC/`) — gnoll model + large Mixamo "sword and shield" animation set, AC_Gnoll, texture
  postprocessor. Not yet wired into Emberwood.
- **Models/Watchtower** — Blender-built tower (`Tools/Blender/build_watchtower.py`) + `WatchtowerSceneSetup.cs`.

## Decisions
- Look: low-poly Gothic 2 style, atmosphere over detail (fog, light, glow, palette).
- Complex assets from user's Midjourney pipeline; Blender only for simple assets.
- Gameplay systems deferred until scene/walk feels right.
- Old Soulslike plan (`guide.txt`) was dropped on 2026-11-14.
