# Emberwood build log — conversation summary

_Covers the working sessions up to 2026-11-14. For the current technical state see `STATE.md`; for a
non-technical description of the scene see `SCENE_BRIEF.md`._

## What was asked for

A first-person Unity scene in a low-poly Gothic 2 style, where the atmosphere comes from light, fog, dark
contrast, glowing objects, foliage and a limited colour palette rather than from detail. The reference was a
set of concept images: an amber, foggy autumn forest with back-lit dark trunks, glowing bulbs, leaf-covered
ground and, later, a dark lake with glowing leaves and flower patches.

Ground rules set by the user:

- Work iteratively in small passes and keep token use low.
- Scene and walking first; all gameplay systems wait.
- The ground must not be a flat plane.
- Simple assets are built in code or Blender; complex ones come from the user (Midjourney, Tripo, Blender).

## How the scene is made

Everything is generated by editor scripts. The menu item `Anthem/Build Emberwood` wipes the scene and rebuilds
it, so hand edits in the scene are lost and changes belong in the scripts. Unity is driven from the shell
through the MCP server (`Tools/Unity/umcp.py`), which is also how screenshots are taken.

## Passes, in order

1. **Blockout.** Terrain with a sunken winding path, procedural trees, bushes, glow bulbs with point lights,
   fireflies, falling leaves, haze, gradient sky, fog and post-processing. Fixed a broken render pipeline
   (everything was magenta) and an old compile error along the way.
2. **Gloomier, 2006 look, more foliage.** Darker grade, film grain, vignette. Undergrowth became thousands of
   alpha cards merged into chunk meshes and placed in patches instead of evenly. Tree branches start higher.
3. **Ground.** Switched to a Unity Terrain with four blended layers (soil, leaf litter, leaf drifts, path).
   The first version looked like one monochrome texture with strange patterns.
4. **Ground fix.** Found the real cause (see "Bugs worth remembering"), replaced the mirrored photo soil with
   seamless procedural dirt, and tinted real leaf sprites in umber, burnt sienna and yellow ochre.
5. **Stones, ferns, large bushes.** The user's 18 stone models are scattered along the path, on the banks and
   in the forest. Ferns grow in their own patches. Dark leaves were removed from the ground. Large bushes are
   domes of many normal-sized cards rather than one stretched card.
6. **Rock texture.** The mirrored rock texture read as brick; replaced with a seamless procedural one.
7. **Lake and flowers.** A shallow lake beside the path with dark water and glowing floating leaf patches,
   plus patches of glowing orange flowers as contrast objects.
8. **Flower shape and spread.** Flowers became flat spiky stars; patches now also appear through the forest.
9. **Moon.** A bright, hard-edged, textured moon disc in the sky; lighting unchanged.
10. **Water.** Custom shader: haze reflection at grazing angles, moving ripples, moon glitter, bulb glints and
    a soft shoreline.

11. **Hanging moss.** Moss strands and vines trail from the tree branches.
12. **Moss thinned, lake enlarged.** Moss is sparse in the forest and heavy around the lake, as single
    cards. The lake is about twice as long, irregular, with an island carrying a bush and a low thick tree
    on the shore.
13. **Mushrooms.** The crystal glow bulbs became simple glowing mushrooms, with extra clusters in the forest.

## Decisions

- Atmosphere over detail; low-poly silhouettes with small, point-filtered textures.
- Unity Terrain for the ground so the user can also hand-paint it.
- Ground and rock textures are generated by scripts in `Tools/Textures/` so the palette can be changed and
  re-run; Midjourney ground textures were dropped because they were blobby and did not tile.
- The user is happy with the ground lighting; it should stay as it is.
- Texture filtering stays on Point for now; the user postponed that decision.
- Landmarks were postponed early on and are now back on the list.

## Bugs worth remembering

- **`Mathf.SmoothStep` is not shader `smoothstep`.** Using it that way produced garbage terrain layer weights,
  so the ground was 100% soil. Use `EmberTerrain.Ss(edge0, edge1, x)`.
- **Mirror-tiling a photo makes patterns.** It caused the ploughed-field soil and the brick-like rock.
- **Height alone does not mean "in the lake".** The south of the map lies below the lake's water level, so a
  height-only check stripped the spawn area of trees and foliage. `Wet()` now also checks the lake basin.
- **Do not rebuild while Unity is in Play mode.** The rebuild is discarded when Play mode ends.
- **Update `STATE.md` with every commit.** It was skipped once and the user had to ask.

## Open items

- Enlarging the forest. Current map is 180 x 180 m; the suggestion is 360 x 360 m with a world-edge boundary
  and possibly a forking path.
- A landmark.
- Trees are still procedural placeholders; unused sprites remain (tall herb stalks, bare twig, leafy branch).
- Large bushes read as dark silhouettes; the rock texture is soft.
- Play mode has never been checked by the assistant: walking, wading, rock colliders and frame rate with
  about 110 point lights.
